小怪 · HD-2D art, batches 1 and 2

Every sprite below is a grid of palette characters, drawn as code. Batch 2 adds villager ladders, six more monster families, two more Undead lines, walk and attack cycles, props, water and a night palette. The sprites are pure pixel art (nearest-neighbour); the lighting, bloom, tilt-shift and grading in the scene are canvas post effects on top.

## Description (paste into the big text box)

**Evolve or Fall** is a fast pixel-art survival game. You are Xiao Guai (小怪), a very small monster who crawls out of a
blood pit at the edge of a very large village that wants you gone. Bite, roll and grow. Every kill feeds you, every level
makes you stronger, and at level 10 you pick one of **nine evolution lines** and begin to change into something much bigger.

But you are not the only one growing.

### Your rival
The village has its own champion. A few seconds into every run a rival hero rises in its own nest far away, on an
evolution line of its own, and comes hunting. It feeds on the creatures *you* defeat and levels just like you: a new skill
every 3rd level, and every 12th level a special power (more health and damage) instead. Hurt it badly and it runs away to
mend. Defeat it and it is back 30 seconds later, as strong as ever. At the 20-minute mark it is the **final duel**: no more
second chances, and one of you falls.

### What you do
- **Bite and roll.** Biting is automatic. The **roll** is your dodge *and* your attack: it hits every enemy you pass
  through. Get hit while rolling for a **perfect dodge**: time slows, the roll is ready again, and your next bite counters
  for double damage.
- **Chain combos.** Keep hitting and a big combo counter takes over the screen: NICE, GREAT, BRUTAL, SAVAGE, UNSTOPPABLE...
  Long chains raise your bite and pay out bones.
- **Evolve** through the 10 forms of your line: Zombie to Vampire Lord, Skeleton to Lich, Hatchling to Elder Dragon,
  Wolf Pup to Fenrir, Seed to World Tree, Imp to Demon Lord and more. Most lines have a signature ability, and every line
  has nine skills of its own.
- **Pick skills, upgrades and specials.** A skill every 3rd level (Frost Shards, Falling Star, Chain Lightning, Thorn Burst,
  Spirit Lance and many more), an upgrade card every 4th, and a special power every 12th (Phoenix, Blood Moon, Gravity Maw...).
- **Explore a big map** with five wild biomes around the green meadow: the Frozen Waste, the Bone Desert, the Murk Swamp,
  the Hollow Graveyard and the Ember Woods. Find shrines (a buff), healing springs, crypts (an ambush and a chest) and ruined
  towers (bones and a view of the map). Rain, snow flurries and fog roll in now and then.
- **Build** bone towers and walls to hold the line, and open treasure chests (watch for mimics).
- **Survive events** like meteor showers, earthquakes, blood moons, raids and stampedes, plus a wandering merchant, the odd
  UFO and a chicken rebellion.
- **Face the bosses.** Hidden bosses gather in the big castle, call guards, enrage and open a weak point. At 15 minutes
  **GOD** descends: it cannot be slowed, burned, rooted, drowned or killed in one blow.

### Controls
| | Desktop | Phone / tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys, or hold the left mouse button | Floating joystick (drag anywhere on the left) |
| Bite | Automatic | Automatic |
| Roll (dodge + hit) | Space or right-click | Tap ROLL |
| Sprint | Hold Shift | Hold ROLL |
| Build / remove | E / X (1 and 2 or B pick tower or wall) | BUILD, SWAP and REMOVE buttons |
| Pick a card | Click, or press 1, 2, 3... | Tap the card |
| Try again | R | AGAIN button |

On a phone every button is labelled, the level-up cards are short with a big icon, the HUD and minimap sit compactly at the
top, and the first run shows quick hints (tap **?** to see them again). Tap **TAP TO START** once so the browser allows sound.

### Tips
- Keep moving: standing still is how a swarm catches you.
- Roll *through* a crowd, not away from it: the roll hits everything it passes.
- The blood pit you start on slowly mends you while you stand at its rim.
- Watch the minimap: the red marker is your rival.
- Best in Chrome or Edge. The first load is about 12 MB; the rest of the music (about 14 MB) downloads in the background
  while the first track plays.

**Title:** Evolve or Fall is playable in your browser

Xiao Guai is a tiny monster with a big problem: a whole village wants him gone, and a rival hero grows stronger on every
creature he defeats.

What is in this build:
- 9 evolution lines with 10 forms each, from Zombie to Vampire Lord and Hatchling to Elder Dragon
- A rival hero that levels like you, runs to heal, comes back, and meets you in a final duel at 20 minutes

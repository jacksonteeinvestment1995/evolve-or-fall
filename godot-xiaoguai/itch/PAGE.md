## Description (paste into the big text box)

**Evolve or Fall** is a fast pixel-art survival game. You are Xiao Guai, a very small monster in a very large village that
wants you gone. Bite, dodge and grow. Every kill feeds you, every level makes you stronger, and at level 10 you choose one of
nine evolution lines and begin to change into something much bigger.

But you are not the only one growing.

### Your rival
The village has its own champion: a rival hero who picks an evolution line of its own every run, levels up from the
creatures *you* defeat, learns skills, evolves, and starts hunting you from the first seconds. Hurt it badly and it runs
away to heal. Defeat it and it returns in 30 seconds, stronger. At the 20-minute mark it is the final duel, and one of you falls.

### What you do
- **Bite, roll and lunge** your way through a village of hundreds. Chain hits into combos, time a roll right for a **perfect dodge** (time slows, your next bite is a counter).
- **Evolve** through 10 forms of the line you pick: Zombie to Vampire Lord, Skeleton to Lich, Hatchling to Elder Dragon, Seed to World Tree and more. Every line has its own signature ability and its own skills.
- **Pick skills and perks** as you level: ranged skills (Frost Shards, Falling Star, Chain Lightning, Thorn Burst, Spirit Lance), healing and defence, and a special skill at every 12th level.
- **Build** bone towers and walls to hold the line, and open treasure chests (watch for mimics).
- **Survive events** like meteor showers, earthquakes, raids and stampedes, plus the odd UFO and a chicken rebellion.
- **Face the bosses.** Hidden bosses hide in the castle and call guards, enrage and open a weak point. At 15 minutes **GOD** descends: it cannot be slowed, burned, rooted, drowned or killed in one blow.

### Tips
- Keep moving: standing still is how a swarm catches you.
- A **perfect dodge** (get hit during a roll) refunds your roll and doubles your next bite.
- Watch the minimap: the red marker with a gold frame is your rival.
- The first music track may start a moment before the rest finish downloading.

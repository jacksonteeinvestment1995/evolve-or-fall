## Design notes

- **Tiers:** stages 1–2 Tier 1 (cheap), 3–5 Tier 2, 6–8 Tier 3, 9–10 Tier 4 (boss-level, full polish).
- **Reuse art:** recolor shared bodies (Fire/Magma/Molten Slime share one sprite).
- **Skip stages by branch:** a run might go Skeleton (1), Skeleton Knight (4), Lich (10); about 5 forms per run.
- **Build one line first:** Undead line 1 (Zombie → Vampire Lord) as the vertical slice.
- **Evolution triggers:** level, item (Fire Stone, Moon Shard, Holy Water), devour 10 monsters of a family, place or time (graveyard at night), synthesis (fuse with another monster).
- **Final boss depends on what 小怪 ate:** mostly Undead → Death Lord, Dragon → Elder Dragon, Slime → Slime Emperor, mixed → hybrid "Chaos Lord" with a unique fight.
- **Element chart (draft):** Fire > Ice > Wind > Earth > Thunder > Fire; Light and Dark beat each other.
- **Family traits (draft):** Undead heal at night, Dragons resist their own element, Slimes can split.
- **Rare branches:** Metal Slime and Treasure Demon are low-HP, high-reward enemies.

小怪 Pixel Sheet

All art for the game in 2D pixel art: five evolution forms, enemies, items, tiles and three sample scenes. Every sprite is a character grid on a 16-colour palette, so new ones are easy to add.

Evolution line

Same eyes and horns in every form. The canvas grows with each stage, so size is part of the story.

The heroes and villagers the player starts out afraid of.

Tiles

Scenes

Three moments from the game, assembled from the pieces above.

Palette and rules

  • Canvas sizes: 16 (stage 1–2), 24 (stage 3), 32 (stage 4–5). Export at 1x, scale in the engine with nearest-neighbour filtering.
  • Every sprite has a 1 px dark outline in the same colour (K). Light comes from the top left, so highlights sit top left and shade sits bottom right.
  • Never rotate or scale by a non-integer amount. Keep one pixel size across the whole game.
  • Animate with 2–4 frames per action (idle, walk, attack) by editing the grid rows, not by stretching.
